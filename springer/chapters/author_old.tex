\chapter*{About the Author} 
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Author}

\textbf{Chris Kuo} is a data science practitioner, AI educator, and consultant with more than 25 years of experience in analytics and quantitative modeling. His work centers on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven decision making. 
He has led data science initiatives in enterprise environments and is the inventor of a U.S. patent for data-driven analytical solutions.

In addition to his industry work, Chris teaches at Columbia University and has previously taught at Boston University, the University of New Hampshire, and Liberty University. His teaching and research interests include AI and machine learning, economics, and management. He has published research in economics and management journals and is the author of 10 books on applied AI, machine learning, and analytics. 

He holds a Ph.D. in Economics from the State University of New York at Stony Brook and a B.S. in Nuclear Engineering from National Tsing Hua University in Taiwan. He currently resides in New York City with his wife.
