\infoverdict
  {One careful study found that a transformer prices U.S. stocks better than factor models and earlier machine-learning models out of sample. Its value after costs and among large stocks has not been tested, and no one has yet reproduced the result independently.}
  {Read Sharpe ratios above three as research results, not as returns, and treat ``AI-powered'' as a claim to test after costs among stocks you can trade.}
